How do I apply?

We like keeping our application process simple, but effective hence our choices.

1

Initial Application ~30 Minutes

Initial information about you, and your project

2

Invitational Interview ~15 mins

Interesting projects and founders are invited to an interview.

3

Detailed Plan ~60 mins

Request for more details about your project, and it's roadmap

4

Offer Extended

If we like your project, we'll extend you an offer to join the fellowship and receive your grant.
